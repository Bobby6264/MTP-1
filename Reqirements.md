* **`processed_gene.csv`:** The columns represent specific **genes** (like *TP53* or  *BRCA1* ). The numbers measure  **mRNA expression levels** , showing how actively the tumor is transcribing those specific genes into proteins.
* **`processed_mirna.csv`:** The columns represent **microRNAs** (like `hsa-let-7a-5p`). The numbers measure the expression of these small, non-coding RNA molecules that regulate and suppress the mRNA.
* **`processed_methylation.csv`:** The columns represent **CpG sites** or genomic regions. The numbers measure  **DNA methylation** , which is a chemical tag added directly to the DNA structure that can permanently silence a gene.
